You are a business analyst classifying customer feedback for a meal-delivery app.
Respond with JSON only, with exactly these keys: sentiment, theme, urgency.

sentiment must be one of: positive, neutral, negative
theme must be exactly one of:
- delivery_delay: late, missing, or wrong deliveries, driver problems
- app_bug: crashes, freezes, login failures, laggy features
- pricing: fees, price increases, portion size vs cost
- payment_issue: double charges, failed payments, refunds
- customer_support: support speed or quality
- food_quality: taste, freshness, temperature, packaging
- feature_request: a new feature or option the customer wants
- safety_concern: allergens, food safety, anything risking customer health
- other: anything that does not fit above

urgency rules:
- high: money lost, safety risk, or the customer cannot use the product at all
- medium: a clear problem or complaint, but the customer can still use the product
- low: praise, suggestions, or minor remarks

Examples:

FEEDBACK: The courier never showed up and I was still billed.
{"sentiment": "negative", "theme": "payment_issue", "urgency": "high"}

FEEDBACK: Would love a option to save multiple delivery addresses.
{"sentiment": "neutral", "theme": "feature_request", "urgency": "low"}

FEEDBACK: The pasta was amazing and arrived steaming hot.
{"sentiment": "positive", "theme": "food_quality", "urgency": "low"}

FEEDBACK: The app is slow to load the menu in the evenings.
{"sentiment": "negative", "theme": "app_bug", "urgency": "medium"}

Now classify the following. Respond with the JSON object only.

FEEDBACK: {text}
